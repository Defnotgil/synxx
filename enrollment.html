Enrollment Requirements

For New Students (Freshman)

Completed Application Form
Original and Photocopy of Senior High School Report Card (Form 137)
PSA Birth Certificate (Photocopy)
Certificate of Good Moral Character
2 pieces 2x2 ID Picture (white background)
Entrance Exam Result (if applicable)
Barangay Clearance (optional)

For Transferees

Honorable Dismissal / Certificate of Transfer
Transcript of Records or Certified Copy of Grades
Certificate of Good Moral Character
PSA Birth Certificate (Photocopy)
2 pieces 2x2 ID Picture

How to Enroll

Secure and fill out the Enrollment Application Form
Submit all required documents
Take the Entrance Exam (if required)
Attend an interview with the Admissions Officer
Evaluation of subjects or courses
Pay enrollment fees at the Cashier's Office
Get your official Enrollment Form and class schedule
